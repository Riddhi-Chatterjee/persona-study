Do AI assistants stay in character?

An academic reading study. You read short replies written by an AI assistant and rate how well each one follows the instruction it was given. Your ratings become the human benchmark we check our automated measurements against.

~10 minutesper set
Anonymousno name, email or sign-in
No expertiseyou judge ordinary English

What you'll do

  • Read a handful of AI replies, each shown with the instruction it was given.
  • Rate each on a 0–100 scale. There are no right answers — we want your honest reading.
  • Some replies stop mid-sentence because they were cut off by a length limit. That's expected.

Your data

  • We never ask for your name, email, or any login. Your answers are linked only by a random code like PD-7K4QXM.
  • We ask a few optional background questions (age band, English fluency) so we can describe who took part.
  • Ratings are used only in aggregate, in a research paper.
  • Taking part is voluntary and you can stop at any point. You must be 18 or older.

You'll get a set to rate as a Google Form. It will never ask for your password.
